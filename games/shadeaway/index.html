Night Arcade

SHADEAWAY

A midnight lattice of glowing shade-cubes. Tap to drift one into the dark when its arrow path is clear — or the lattice remembers. Drag to turn the sky.

How to play

  • Drag with one finger to rotate the lattice. Spin it often — clear paths hide on the back and sides.
  • Tap a shade-cube to send it drifting along its arrow. It only leaves if every cell ahead on that ray is empty.
  • A blocked tap costs a life. Lose them all and the lattice soft-locks — Retry, Hint, or Undo from there.
  • Frosted cubes thaw after other cubes have drifted. Clear the warm ones first.
  • Clear every cube to win. Hint lights a safe peel. Undo is unlimited. No paywalls.
