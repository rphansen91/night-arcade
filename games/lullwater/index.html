Night Arcade

LULLWATER

Moonlit keys drift down onto a still black lake. Touch each one as it meets the water and the night plays its lullaby. Miss one, or touch the empty lake, and the music stops.

Choose a song

How to play

  • Tap the glowing keys as they reach the waterline. Each key plays the next note of the song.
  • Never tap the empty lake, and don't let a key sink past the bottom untouched. Either one stops the music.
  • Long keys have a thread of moonlight. Press and hold until the thread runs out for bonus points.
  • Twin keys arrive side by side. Tap both.
  • Finish the song to earn a ★. It starts again, faster, for the second and third stars, then three ♛ crowns, then the lake runs endless.
  • Hitting a key right on the line scores Perfect (2 points). One free Second wind per run lets you carry on after a slip.
