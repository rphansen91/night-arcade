# Stillwater answer key

Right = keep (mine), left = release (not mine), up = split.

## 1. First stream (Stoic)

- **The weather** → RELEASE. Rain keeps its own schedule. Your coat is yours. The sky is not.
- **My reply** → KEEP. Every word you send is yours to choose.
- **My effort today** → KEEP. Effort is one thing no one can give you or take from you.

Lesson: Some things are up to us and some are not. Sorting them is the first freedom.

## 2. The fork (Stoic)

- **Traffic on the way in** → RELEASE. The traffic was there before you. Your patience is what you bring to it.
- **When I leave the house** → KEEP. Leaving ten minutes earlier is entirely yours.
- **Getting older** → RELEASE. Age arrives on its own. How you spend this year is yours.
- **How I treat the waiter** → KEEP. Courtesy is completely your choice.
- **What already happened** → RELEASE. It is done. Only what you do with it now is yours.
- **My next sentence** → KEEP. Nothing is more yours than the next thing you say.
- **Other people's moods** → RELEASE. You can be kind beside a mood, but you can't set it.
- **Keeping my promise** → KEEP. Keeping your word is a choice you make again each day.
- **Whether the train is late** → RELEASE. Timetables belong to the railway. Your book for the wait is yours.

Lesson: Freedom isn't getting the world to agree. It's putting your whole weight on what is yours.

## 3. Their reaction (Communication)

- **He ignored my email** → RELEASE. You can follow up, but his silence is his.
- **One clear follow-up** → KEEP. Writing one kind, clear follow-up is entirely yours.
- **How she hears my tone** → RELEASE. You choose the words. How they land passes through her day, not yours.
- **Choosing my words** → KEEP. Word choice is yours, every single time.
- **Being understood** → SPLIT (keep: Explaining it clearly / release: What they take away). Clarity is your half. What they make of it lives in their head.
- **Whether they agree** → RELEASE. You can make the best case. Agreement is theirs to give.
- **Listening before I answer** → KEEP. Attention is something you can give right now.
- **Apologising for my part** → KEEP. Owning your part is always available to you.
- **Whether they forgive me** → RELEASE. You can apologise well. Forgiveness belongs to them.

Lesson: Your words are yours. Their reaction is theirs. Say it well, then let it land where it lands.

## 4. Clinging (Buddhist)

- **How long this evening lasts** → RELEASE. Good moments end on their own. Gripping them spoils them.
- **Savouring this moment** → KEEP. Attention to right now is yours.
- **Being liked by everyone** → RELEASE. No one controls being liked by everyone. Let it float.
- **Being kind anyway** → KEEP. Kindness doesn't need anyone's approval to be yours.
- **The way things used to be** → RELEASE. The past is a stone that has already sunk.
- **My relationship** → SPLIT (keep: How I show up / release: How they feel). You bring your half: presence and care. Their heart is theirs.
- **Noticing my breath** → KEEP. Your breath is the oldest thing that is yours.
- **Needing it to go my way** → RELEASE. Outcomes depend on many hands. The needing is the part that hurts.
- **Putting down a grudge** → KEEP. Only you can set a grudge down. That practice is yours.

Lesson: Craving wants the river to stop. Peace lets it flow while you tend what is yours.

## 5. The boardroom (Strategy)

- **Interest rates** → RELEASE. Rates move with the economy. Your plan for them is yours.
- **Our pricing** → KEEP. You set your own prices.
- **A competitor's launch** → RELEASE. Their roadmap is theirs. Your response is yours.
- **Our product quality** → KEEP. Quality is built by your team's daily choices.
- **Hitting the quarter** → SPLIT (keep: Pipeline and effort / release: The final number). You drive the inputs. The result also depends on buyers and timing.
- **Which customer to call first** → KEEP. Prioritising is fully yours.
- **The market's mood today** → RELEASE. Markets swing on millions of moods. Don't trade your plan for one day.
- **Whether the investor says yes** → RELEASE. You control the pitch, not the decision.
- **How fast we answer support** → KEEP. Response time is a choice of staffing and priority.

Lesson: Strategy is mostly choosing what not to worry about. Pour effort where your hands can reach.

## 6. Sunk cost (Strategy)

- **The year already spent** → RELEASE. That time is gone whatever you choose next.
- **Whether to continue from here** → KEEP. The forward decision is yours.
- **Money already paid** → RELEASE. Spent money can't be unspent. Only future money is still in play.
- **What I'd choose fresh today** → KEEP. That is the right question, and it is yours.
- **Our struggling project** → SPLIT (keep: What we do next / release: What we already put in). The past investment is spent. The next step is still yours.
- **The ticket I already bought** → RELEASE. It is spent whether you go or not. Go only if tonight is still worth it.
- **Cancelling the subscription** → KEEP. One tap, entirely yours.
- **Looking foolish for quitting** → RELEASE. What others make of your U-turn is theirs. Saving face can cost more than changing.
- **Asking for an honest review** → KEEP. Inviting a fresh pair of eyes is yours to do.

Lesson: Past costs are already downstream. Decide only from where you stand.

## 7. Signal and noise (Strategy)

- **A rumour about layoffs** → RELEASE. Unconfirmed talk isn't yours to act on. Wait for facts.
- **What customers asked for** → KEEP. Customer requests are signal you can act on.
- **Today's stock price** → RELEASE. Daily swings are mostly noise you can't move.
- **Our churn numbers** → KEEP. Churn is signal. It points at things you can fix.
- **An angry stranger's comment** → RELEASE. One stranger's anger is noise, not a mandate.
- **A harsh online review** → SPLIT (keep: The fixable point in it / release: The tone it was written in). Take the fixable point. The tone was theirs.
- **My manager's feedback** → KEEP. Direct feedback from someone close to the work is signal worth acting on.
- **A viral hot take** → RELEASE. Hot takes are built to spread, not to guide you.
- **How I slept this week** → KEEP. Your sleep is a signal about you, and you can act on it.

Lesson: Attention is your scarcest budget. Spend it only on what you can act on.

## 8. Still water (All)

- **Whether the flight is delayed** → RELEASE. The airline and the weather decide that.
- **Packing a book for the wait** → KEEP. Preparing for the wait is entirely yours.
- **My boss's opinion of me** → SPLIT (keep: My work / release: What she concludes). You shape it with your work. Her conclusions are still hers.
- **How I spend my evening** → KEEP. Your evening is yours to spend.
- **What happened at school** → RELEASE. That chapter is already written.
- **Praise from strangers** → RELEASE. Praise is pleasant weather. Let it pass too.
- **My health** → SPLIT (keep: Sleep, food, movement / release: Genes and luck). You steer the habits. Some of it is luck.
- **Asking for help** → KEEP. Asking is always in your hands.
- **Who wins the election** → RELEASE. Your vote is yours. The total is millions of other choices.

Lesson: Muddy water, let stand, becomes clear. Keep what is yours and let the rest flow.
